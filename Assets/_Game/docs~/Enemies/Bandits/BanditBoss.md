# Bandit Boss

The self-proclaimed King of the Outskirts. Imposing, scarred, and wielding a massive jagged blade, he leads his group with a mix of charisma and brutal force.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Melee (Heavy)
- **Danger Level**: High

## Abilities
- **Executioner's Swing**: A heavy overhead strike that can stun smaller units.
- **Warlord's Command**: Roars, briefly increasing the movement speed of nearby bandits.

## Lore
"Power is taken, never given. I took this land, and I'll take your life if you stand in my way." — The Outskirts King

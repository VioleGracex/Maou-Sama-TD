# Bandit Melee

The rank-and-file of the borderlands' criminal underworld. These are desperate men and women who have turned to banditry to survive the chaos of the shifting kingdoms.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Melee
- **Danger Level**: Low

## Abilities
- **Rough Slash**: A simple but effective sword strike with a rusted blade.

## Lore
"They aren't born evil. Just hungry. But a hungry man with a sword is just as dangerous as a demon." — Ignis

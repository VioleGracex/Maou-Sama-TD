# Wolf Rider

A deadly combination of a trained bandit and a wild dire wolf. These scouts excel at flanking maneuvers and striking where the enemy is most vulnerable.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human / Beast
- **Type**: Ground Melee (Fast)
- **Danger Level**: Medium

## Abilities
- **Pounce**: The wolf leaps forward, closing the gap instantly.
- **Twin Strike**: The rider slashes while the wolf bites, dealing damage twice.

## Lore
"Don't look at the man. Watch the wolf. The man wants your gold; the wolf just wants your throat." — Ravenna

# Cleric of Light

Devout priests who provide spiritual support to the Crusade. They do not attack directly; instead, they channel holy energy to heal wounded crusaders and project light shields to guard them against our magic.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Ranged (Support)
- **Danger Level**: Medium

## Abilities
- **Holy Restoration**: Heals the most damaged adjacent Crusader unit for 40 HP every 2 seconds.
- **Aegis of the Sun**: Places a barrier on a nearby ally, making them immune to negative status effects and reducing magic damage taken by 30% for 3 seconds.

## Lore
"Their prayers sound beautiful, but their light is cold. It sears our flesh like acid." — Eidon

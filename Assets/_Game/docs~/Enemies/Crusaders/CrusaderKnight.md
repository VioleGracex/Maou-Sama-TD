# Crusader Knight

The holy vanguard of the Sanctus Alliance. These soldiers are fueled by fanatical devotion to the Light, marching in gleaming silver plate armor decorated with sunburst crests, and wielding blessed longswords that burn our demonic units.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Melee
- **Danger Level**: Low

## Abilities
- **Holy Smite**: Deals minor bonus magic (Light) damage to demonic or undead units.
- **Sunburst Shield**: Gains a temporary shield that absorbs 50 damage when deploying.

## Lore
"They call us devils, monsters, shadows. Yet they march with torches to burn our homes, calling their fires holy." — Malina

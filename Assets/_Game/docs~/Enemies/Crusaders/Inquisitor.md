# Inquisitor

Fanatical hunters of the Crusade. Wielding dual light-infused bows, they attack at extreme speed from range, firing arrows of concentrated solar energy to melt our defenses and target our backline.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Ranged
- **Danger Level**: Medium

## Abilities
- **Light Barrage**: Attacks twice in rapid succession every 3rd shot.
- **Mark of the Sinner**: Marks the target, increasing damage taken from all Crusader units by 15% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
"They don't want to conquer us. They want to purify us. And to them, purification means ashes." — Astaroth

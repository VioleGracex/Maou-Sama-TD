# Saintess of Light

The chosen oracle of the Sanctus God, and the supreme leader of the Crusade. Hovering above the ground, she channels pure solar rays through her legendary staff, dealing massive AoE magic damage, blinding our units, and granting temporary invincibility to her crusaders.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human (Blessed)
- **Type**: Hovering Ranged (Boss)
- **Danger Level**: Boss

## Abilities
- **Solar Flare**: Summons a pillar of holy light that deals massive magic damage to a 3x3 area and blinds affected player units for 3 seconds (making them miss attacks).
- **Heavenly Aegis**: Grants a temporary invincibility shield to all adjacent Crusader units for 2 seconds (triggered at 50% HP).

## Lore
"She smiles like a mother, but she commands the destruction of our entire race. Do not let her gentle face fool you; she is the most dangerous blade the heavens have ever forged." — Balthazar

# Templar Champion

Elite giant templars carrying massive light-infused halberds. Equipped with heavy silver-gold full-plate armor, they march relentlessly, dealing sweeping area-of-effect damage and breaking through our lines.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Heavy
- **Danger Level**: High

## Abilities
- **Divine Sweep**: Attacks in a 3-tile horizontal line, dealing physical and light damage to all hit units.
- **Unwavering Faith**: Reduces all incoming crowd control (stuns, slows) duration by 50%.

## Lore
"When they swing their halberds, they claim the light guides their strike. Let's see if their faith can block a volley of shadow arrows." — Ranger Vaelin

# Guild Alchemist

Underworld alchemists contracted by the Guild. Wielding glass flasks filled with volatile chemical mixtures, they throw explosive fire and acid onto our lines, dealing magic damage over time to multiple units.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Ranged
- **Danger Level**: Medium

## Abilities
- **Volatile Mixture**: Throws a flask dealing fire damage in a 3x3 grid around the target, leaving a burning area for 3 seconds.
- **Corrosive Catalyst**: Reduces the physical defense of affected units by 25% for 4 seconds.

## Lore
"They smell of sulfur, brimstone, and cheap spirits. Keep them away from our supplies unless you want everything to go up in flames." — Eidon

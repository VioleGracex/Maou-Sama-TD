# Guild Crossbowman

Ranged support units equipped with heavy mechanical crossbows designed by the Guild's engineers. They deploy on elevated positions or behind frontlines to pierce the armor of our vanguard units.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Ranged
- **Danger Level**: Low

## Abilities
- **Armor Piercing Bolt**: Ranged attack that ignores 15% of the target's physical defense.

## Lore
"Their crossbows are heavy, slow, and expensive. But they get the job done when it comes to keeping our legions at bay." — Malina

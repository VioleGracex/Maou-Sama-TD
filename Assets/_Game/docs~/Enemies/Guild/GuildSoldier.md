# Guild Soldier

The standard melee infantry of the Merchant Guild's private army. Clad in sturdy breastplates and armed with short steel swords, they coordinate in tight formations to protect their merchants' trade routes and seize disputed territories.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Melee
- **Danger Level**: Low

## Abilities
- **Shield Bash**: Stuns the target for 0.5 seconds on strike.
- **Formation slash**: Deals slightly increased physical damage when adjacent to another Guild Soldier.

## Lore
"Do not underestimate them just because they fight for coin. A soldier who fights for gold is cheap, but their steel is still sharp enough to kill." — Balthazar

# Guild Warlord

The supreme commander of the Guild's iron legions. Equipped with a high-tech electrostatic heavy hammer and clad in custom-fitted gilded plate armor, the Warlord leads from the front, buffing nearby troops and dealing heavy electric damage.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Heavy (Boss)
- **Danger Level**: Boss

## Abilities
- **Electrostatic Smash**: Strikes the ground, dealing massive physical damage to the primary target and chaining lightning to up to 3 adjacent allies.
- **Warlord's Command**: Increases the attack power and move speed of all nearby Guild units by 20%.

## Lore

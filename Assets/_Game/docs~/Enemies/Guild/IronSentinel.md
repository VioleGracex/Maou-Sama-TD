# Iron Sentinel

An elite heavy guardian fitted with thick iron plating and carrying a massive tower shield. The Iron Sentinel's main objective is to block narrow corridors and absorb ranged damage, allowing the Guild's damage-dealers to advance safely.

## Profile
- **Race**: Human
- **Type**: Ground Heavy
- **Danger Level**: Medium

## Abilities
- **Phalanx Barrier**: Reduces all incoming ranged damage by 40% from the front.
- **Unyielding Stance**: Immune to knockback and stun effects.

## Lore
"You can't move them, and you can't shoot through them. You have to find a way to melt them from the inside." — Malina

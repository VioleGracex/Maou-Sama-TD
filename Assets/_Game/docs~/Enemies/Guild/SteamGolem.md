# Steam Golem

A massive mechanical automaton powered by compressed steam and burning core-ore. Built by Guild engineers to break through fortress walls, this heavy machine deals devastating damage with its brass fists but moves very slowly.

## Profile
- **Race**: Automaton
- **Type**: Ground Heavy
- **Danger Level**: Medium

## Abilities
- **Steam Discharge**: Emits a blast of scalding steam that deals minor magic damage to all adjacent player units every 4 seconds.
- **Heavy Brass Strike**: Deals high physical damage and knocks back the blocker.

## Lore
"Brass, coal, and arrogance. That is all it takes for the humans to construct these machines. Still, they burn hot and hit hard." — Ignis

# Kingdom of Drakmora

The Kingdom of Drakmora is a vast, highly diverse superpower situated at the very center of the Aethelgard continent. Rather than being a strictly dark or volcanic wasteland, its central location grants it extreme ecological diversity. It is divided into several unique territories, each governed by different factions of the Demon Army.

## Territories & Terrain

### 1. The Crownlands (Central)
The capital and seat of the Maou's power. It uniquely blends imposing obsidian architecture with vibrant, overgrown flora and flowing mana rivers. It serves as the grand nexus connecting all other biomes of the kingdom.

### 2. The Frostbound Expanse (North)
A punishing but beautiful northern tundra covered in perpetual snowstorms, glaciers, and frozen lakes. Giant, ancient evergreens form dense alpine forests that serve as the hunting grounds for Shifters and Ice-beasts. 

### 3. The Scorched Sands (South)
A vast, unyielding desert environment featuring rolling dunes, ancient sandstone ruins, and hidden oasis cities. This harsh environment breeds uniquely resilient warriors and serves as a natural barrier to the southern empires.

### 4. The Whispering Wilds (East)
Endless, dense green forests intertwined with deep lakes and winding rivers. Glowing flora, massive ancient trees, and deep magic saturate this area, providing lumber and natural fortresses for the kingdom.

### 5. The Azure Coast (West)
A sprawling coastline meeting a vast, deep-water ocean. It features prosperous naval ports, sandy beaches, and treacherous archipelagos. Naval merchants, deep-sea familiars, and fleet commanders operate here to secure the maritime borders.

### 6. The Deep Vaults (Underground)
A subterranean network of massive caverns stretching beneath the entire kingdom. It features glowing crystal formations, underground lakes, and industrial foundries populated by Corrupted Dwarves and Dark Elves.

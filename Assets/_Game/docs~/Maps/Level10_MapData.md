# Level 10: The Throne of Gehenna

**Location:** The Throne Room
**Grid Size:** 15 (Width) x 15 (Height)

## 1. Atmosphere & Lighting
*   **Atmosphere:** The grand climax. A collision of absolute holy power and absolute demonic corruption. The Saintess of Light is defending the throne.
*   **Lighting (Global):** A blinding, dramatic shaft of pure white/gold holy light from the Saintess's barrier hits the center, clashing with the dark purple demonic energy radiating from the edges.
*   **Lighting (Local/Point):** The Throne itself glows with intense dark purple magic as the Maou approaches to reclaim it.
*   **Fog:** A clash of mists: bright, sparkling golden holy mist fighting against a thick, swirling dark purple demonic fog.

## 2. Tile Specifications (1:1 Unity Cubes)
*   **Walkable Path Tiles (Maze):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** A highly reflective, grand white and gold marble checkered floor.
    *   **Decorations:** Deep, corrupted purple cracks spreading through the pristine holy marble. Shattered stained glass on the floor.
*   **Unwalkable Tiles (Obstacles):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Massive fluted marble columns.
    *   **Decorations:** The Grand Throne on a raised dais. Some columns are wrapped in holy light, others are corrupted by demonic black obsidian spikes.

## 3. Map Layout
*   **Spawn Point:** (1, 7) - The grand entrance.
*   **Exit Points:** (13, 3) and (13, 11) - Paths leading behind the throne dais.
*   **Structure:** A maze leading up the grand hall towards the throne area, representing the final defensive lines of the Crusade.

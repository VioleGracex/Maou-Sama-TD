# Level 1: Chapter 1: The Awakening

**Location:** The Obsidian Sanctuary (Tomb of the Sovereign)
**Grid Size:** 15 (Width) x 9 (Height)

## 1. Atmosphere & Lighting
*   **Atmosphere:** The ancient resting place of the Demon Lord. Cold, oppressive, yet pulsing with awakening dark magic.
*   **Lighting (Global):** Desaturated, pitch-black shadows pierced by a deep, glowing ambient violet light. 
*   **Lighting (Local/Point):** Floating embers of fiery orange and cursed violet drift slowly through the air.
*   **Fog:** A dense, thick, rolling **violet fog** that completely swallows the areas outside the main path, swirling around the ancient tombs.

## 2. Tile Specifications (1:1 Unity Cubes)
*   **Walkable Path Tiles (Single Line Path):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** A single, narrow winding line of cracked, dusty stone tiles.
    *   **Decorations:** Scattered bones, ancient dust.
*   **Unwalkable Tiles (Obstacles and Void):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Massive, towering stacks of ancient stone coffins and sarcophagi.
    *   **Decorations:** The coffins act as the walls of the level, jutting out of the thick violet fog.

## 3. Map Layout
*   **Spawn Point:** (14, 2)
*   **Exit Point:** (5, 4)
*   **Structure:** A single, narrow, winding line path. Enemies are forced down this singular claustrophobic corridor.

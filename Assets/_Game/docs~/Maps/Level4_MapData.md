# Level 4: The Black Walls

**Location:** Outer Perimeter of the Capital (Siege Camp)
**Grid Size:** 15 (Width) x 15 (Height)

## 1. Atmosphere & Lighting
*   **Atmosphere:** A militaristic siege perimeter. Gilder's Mercenary vanguard has set up camp near the towering black walls of the human capital.
*   **Lighting (Global):** Overcast, gloomy daylight or stormy twilight. 
*   **Lighting (Local/Point):** Burning mercenary campfires and torches scattered around the blockades.
*   **Fog:** Smoke from the campfires and smoldering ruins.

## 2. Tile Specifications (1:1 Unity Cubes)
*   **Walkable Path Tiles (3 converging paths):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Trampled mud and cobblestone road leading to the gates.
    *   **Decorations:** Broken mercenary weapons, discarded shields, and cart tracks.
*   **Unwalkable Tiles (Obstacles):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Massive black stone defensive walls and wooden palisades.
    *   **Decorations:** Mercenary tents, spiked wooden barricades, and standard banners of the human forces.

## 3. Map Layout
*   **Spawn Points:** (0, 3), (0, 7), (0, 11) - Three distinct paths from the left.
*   **Exit Point:** (14, 7) - Paths merge at the gate on the right.
*   **Structure:** Three lanes converging into one major chokepoint breach.

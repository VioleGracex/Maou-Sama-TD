# Level 5: The Crimson Quarter

**Location:** Adventurer Guild Barracks
**Grid Size:** 15 (Width) x 15 (Height)

## 1. Atmosphere & Lighting
*   **Atmosphere:** A dense, militarized urban sector. The humans have turned this once-grand demonic quarter into a staging ground.
*   **Lighting (Global):** Dusk/sunset, casting long dramatic shadows.
*   **Lighting (Local/Point):** Street lamps and glowing windows from the barracks.
*   **Fog:** Very little fog, just atmospheric city haze.

## 2. Tile Specifications (1:1 Unity Cubes)
*   **Walkable Path Tiles (2 merging paths):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Narrow cobblestone streets.
    *   **Decorations:** Crimson banners of the Adventurer Guild torn on the ground, weapons racks, and wooden crates.
*   **Unwalkable Tiles (Obstacles):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Brick and mortar barracks buildings.
    *   **Decorations:** Guard towers, weapon armories, and high brick walls acting as the maze.

## 3. Map Layout
*   **Spawn Points:** (0, 4), (0, 10) - Two lanes.
*   **Exit Point:** (14, 7) - Lanes converge.
*   **Structure:** Narrow urban streets merging into a central chokepoint.

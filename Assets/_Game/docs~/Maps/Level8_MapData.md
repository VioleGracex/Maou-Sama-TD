# Level 8: The Whispering Vaults

**Location:** The Deep Treasury Vaults
**Grid Size:** 15 (Width) x 15 (Height)

## 1. Atmosphere & Lighting
*   **Atmosphere:** Quiet, rich, and deceptive. The perfect place for assassins to hide in the dark.
*   **Lighting (Global):** Pitch black.
*   **Lighting (Local/Point):** Gleaming piles of gold that reflect the sparse, pale magical lights. 
*   **Fog:** No fog, just inky, absolute darkness in the shadows.

## 2. Tile Specifications (1:1 Unity Cubes)
*   **Walkable Path Tiles (Maze):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Highly polished, dark polished marble that reflects light like a mirror.
    *   **Decorations:** Scattered gold coins, jewels, and dropped daggers.
*   **Unwalkable Tiles (Obstacles):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Massive piles of gold, treasure chests, and thick vault doors.
    *   **Decorations:** Obsidian statues hiding in the dark, heavy iron bars. 

## 3. Map Layout
*   **Spawn Point:** (1, 7)
*   **Exit Points:** (13, 3) and (13, 11)
*   **Structure:** A confusing maze of treasure piles, where shadows are deep and paths wind tightly.

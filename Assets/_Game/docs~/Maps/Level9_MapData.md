# Level 9: The Sovereign Keep

**Location:** The Keep Gates & Bridge
**Grid Size:** 15 (Width) x 15 (Height)

## 1. Atmosphere & Lighting
*   **Atmosphere:** Epic and climactic. The approach to the final sanctum. The Sword Saint stands guard on the grand bridge.
*   **Lighting (Global):** Dawn breaking. A bright, pale golden light mixing with the dark clouds of the demon invasion.
*   **Lighting (Local/Point):** Holy light aura around the keep gates.
*   **Fog:** Morning mist rolling off the edges of the bridge into the moat/void.

## 2. Tile Specifications (1:1 Unity Cubes)
*   **Walkable Path Tiles (Maze/Bridge approach):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Grand, pristine white marble paving stones.
    *   **Decorations:** Holy banners of the Crusade, dropped holy weapons, and clean masonry.
*   **Unwalkable Tiles (Obstacles):**
    *   **Texture/Material:** Keep walls, defensive towers, and bridge railings.
    *   **Decorations:** Statues of previous Sword Saints, heavily fortified gate structures, and holy knight standards.

## 3. Map Layout
*   **Spawn Point:** (1, 7)
*   **Exit Points:** (13, 3) and (13, 11)
*   **Structure:** A maze representing the heavily guarded, zigzagging approach over the grand bridge and through the outer keep walls.

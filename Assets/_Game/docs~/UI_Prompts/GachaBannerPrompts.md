# Gacha Banner AI Prompts & Strategic Categories

This document defines the visual identity and AI generation strategies for the recruitment system. Each category is designed to serve a specific gameplay purpose (Limited, Regional, or Tactical).

## Designer Specifications

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Recommended Asset Sizes:**
> - **Banner Image**: 1024 x 768 (**4:3 Ratio**) - Main Panel.
> - **Tab Image**: 200 x 100 (**2:1 Ratio**) - Selector icon.
> - **Full Panel**: 1450 x 768 (**~1.89:1 Wide**) - Background panels.

---

## Category A: Limited / Rate-Up Banners
**Strategy**: Create "FOMO" and visual awe. Used for the strongest UR/SSR units.

### 1. Banner: Abyssal Ritual (Lords of the Void)
- **Vibe**: Eldritch Majesty, Cosmic Horror, Transcendent Power.
- **Featured Characters**: Lucifer (God of Pride), Satan (God of Wrath).
- **AI Prompt**: 
  > "A museum-quality anime splash art for a Limited Gacha Banner: 'Abyssal Ritual'. In the center, Lucifer and Satan stand within a swirling vortex of dark purple cosmic energy and obsidian shards. Lucifer holds a staff of void-light; Satan brandishes a sword of black flame. The background is a shattered dimension showing a silhouette of the Maou's Castle against a dead sun. Fate Ufotable art style, ultra-detailed ornate armor, cinematic particles, dark fantasy masterpiece, extreme contrast, 4:3 composition."
- **Tab Prompt (2:1)**:
  > "A cinematic close-up profile of Lucifer's face. 2:1 aspect ratio. His silver eyes glow with purple void energy. Ornate obsidian crown partially visible. Background is a dark cosmic gradient. Fate Ufotable style, sharp focus, icons-ready."

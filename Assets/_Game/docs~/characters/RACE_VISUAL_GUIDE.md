# Drakmora Kingdom: Race Visual Guide

This guide details the physical characteristics, general aesthetic build, and standard AI generation prompts for all the major races serving under the Maou within the Drakmora Kingdom. Use this as a reference when designing new characters or generating assets.

---

## 1. Trueborn Demon
The original denizens of the dark realms, Trueborn Demons represent the core nobility and raw magical powerhouse of the Maou's forces.

*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Demonic traits, varying quantities of horns, sharp features, and often crimson or obsidian accents. They project an aura of arrogant nobility and raw power.
*   **Key Details**: Pointed ears, glowing eyes (often red or purple), digitigrade legs or tails depending on specific lineage.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `demonic horns, sharp features, fantasy armor, glowing eyes, dark fantasy setting`

---

## 2. Undead
Reanimated by powerful necromancy or lingering grudges, the Undead range from mindless foot soldiers to hyper-intelligent Liches.

*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Pale, skeletal, or rotting features. Many retain the battered armor they died in, infused with dark magic.
*   **Key Details**: Glowing ethereal eyes (often blue or sickly green), visible bone structure, tattered clothing, and a general aura of decay and coldness.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `undead, pale skin, glowing eyes, dark fantasy, decaying armor, ominous`

---

## 3. Familiar (Monsters & Creatures)
Summoned beasts, abyssal creatures, and magical constructs of flesh and blood that serve various roles from assassins to meat shields.

*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Monstrous or beastly forms ranging from swarms of bats to towering slimes or lesser harpies.
*   **Key Details**: Glowing arcane markings, unnatural proportions, multiple eyes or limbs.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `creature, monster, glowing details, fantasy beast, dark fantasy setting`

---

## 4. Human (and Demon Apostles)
The baseline mortal race. While baseline Humans are standard, **Demon Apostles** are a specific faction of Humans who have defected from Sanctus/The Light to serve Drakmora.

*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Standard human build with highly diverse features. Demon Apostles often wear corrupted Sanctus armor or darker, ragged clothing representing their defection.
*   **Key Details**: Scars, corrupted holy symbols, practical weaponry.
*   **AI Prompt Base (Apostle)**: `human, detailed fantasy clothing, corrupted knight, dark fantasy setting`

---

## 5. Shifter
Tribal warriors from the untamed edges of the continent who harness the power of beasts. 

*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Rugged, muscular builds with beastial traits such as animal ears, tails, fangs, and thick fur.
*   **Key Details**: Feral expressions, leather armor, war paint, and tribal adornments.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `animal ears, bestial features, feral, thick fur, rugged tribal armor, fantasy`

---

## 6. Construct
Mechanical or golem-like entities forged in the deep forges of Drakmora. They are tireless, emotionless engines of war.

*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Made primarily of stone, metal, and animated by a glowing magical core. 
*   **Key Details**: Blocky, massive frames, glowing runic lines, emitting steam or magical exhaust.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `construct, made of stone and metal, glowing core, mecha golem, mechanical parts, fantasy`

---

## 7. The Fallen
Once beings of pure light or angelic soldiers from Sanctus, now cast out or voluntarily corrupted by the Abyss.

*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Beautiful but corrupted features. They retain their majestic wings, but the feathers are pitch black, bloodstained, or bone-like.
*   **Key Details**: Shattered or corrupted halos, dark and elegant robes, weeping black tears or wielding corrupted holy fire.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `fallen angel, black feathered wings, corrupted halo, dark robes, majestic but sinister, fantasy`

---

## 8. Sub-Races & Minor Factions
While the primary races make up the bulk of the Maou's forces, several unique sub-species, mythological hybrids, and anomalous interlopers have also joined the ranks.

### Interloper
Warriors pulled through spatial rifts from other worlds (e.g., Terra Prime).
*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Distinctly out-of-place, usually favoring sci-fi, cyberpunk, or modern tactical aesthetics.
*   **Key Details**: VR goggles, firearms, urban techwear.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `sci-fi techwear, modern soldier in fantasy, VR goggles, tactical gear, interloper`

### Dwarf
Stout subterranean dwellers who bring masterful engineering to the dark forces.
*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Short, wide, heavily muscled and highly bearded. Covered in soot, soot-stained leathers, and heavy metal plating.
*   **Key Details**: Intricate braided beards, glowing forge-runes, massive hammers or blunderbusses.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `dwarf, stout, heavily bearded, muscular, soot-stained, heavy metal armor, fantasy`

### Mythological Hybrid (Dragonoid / Demon)
Rare legendary entities born from the union of apex predator species like dragons and true demons.
*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Imposing, towering over normal humans. They boast prominent dragon scales or immense draconic horns layered with demonic elements.
*   **Key Details**: Scales running up their arms/neck, slitted reptilian eyes, imposing wings or thick tails.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `dragonoid, half-dragon, scaled skin, reptilian eyes, draconic horns, fantasy, imposing`

### Spirit (Cosmic / Elemental)
Ethereal beings unbound by flesh, representing raw elemental or cosmic forces.
*   **Build & Aesthetics**: Forms made entirely of energy, wind, or the night sky itself. Often lack distinct facial features, appearing as living constellations or tempests.
*   **Key Details**: Translucent, glowing bodies, floating without touching the ground, swirling elemental effects.
*   **AI Prompt Base**: `ethereal spirit, translucent body, made of starlight and nebula, cosmic entity, glowing magic`

### Specific Beast & Familiar Sub-Types
Familiar and Shifter ranks are highly varied. Common sub-types include:
*   **Harpies**: Winged humanoids with talons, feathers replacing arms/legs. (`harpy, bird wings, feathered, talons, wild hair`)
*   **Minotaurs**: Towering half-bull powerhouses wielding immense, crude weapons. (`minotaur, bull head, muscular, towering, horned, hooves`)
*   **Goblins**: Small, green or grey-skinned scavengers functioning as stealth units or engineers. (`goblin, green skin, pointed ears, small stature, ragged clothing`)
*   **Slimes**: Gelatinous creatures capable of absorbing impacts, dissolving matter. (`slime creature, gelatinous, translucent body, monster, glowing inside`)

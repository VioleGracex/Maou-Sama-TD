## Lore Fragment
A legendary Guard Captain of Gehenna and a Trueborn Demon Bastion of absolute loyalty. When the 13th Maou fell fifty years ago, Ignis refused to surrender or retreat. She stayed behind to guard the entrance of the awakening chamber in the Ashen Wastes, keeping watch alone for five decades until her Sovereign's resurrection. Standing as an unbreakable bulwark, she wields a massive, fiery red greatsword, commands Gehenna's frontline defense, and manipulates devastating fiery fire in combat.

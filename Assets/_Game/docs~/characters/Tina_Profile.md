## Lore & Personality
Tina is the primary advisor, right hand, and strategic architect of the Demon King's forces. Cold, analytical, and fiercely loyal, she was the one who managed the remaining remnants of Gehenna's defenses during the Maou's long fifty-year slumber. She combines ancient demonic sorcery with high-caliber tactical precision, utilizing a glowing cyan tactical tablet that projects a "Spectral Map" to track environmental sensors, soul signals, and enemy patrol structures. During the awakening, she successfully managed the tomb's reactivation and continues to coordinate all movement, scouting paths, and tactical layouts for the Sovereign's reclamation campaign.

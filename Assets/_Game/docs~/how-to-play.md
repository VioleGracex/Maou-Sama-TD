# How to Play: Maou-Sama TD

Welcome to **Maou-Sama TD**, a reverse Tower Defense where you play as the 13th Demon Lord awakened from an eternal slumber. The humans have invaded your domain, and it is your job to command your demonic vassals to repel the crusade and retake your capital.

This guide compiles everything you need to know about navigating the game, managing your forces, and dominating the battlefield.

---

## 1. The Core Loop (How You Progress)
Your journey is managed from the **Citadel (Home)** hub. The overarching gameplay loop is as follows:

1.  **Conquest (Campaign):** Enter the Conquest map to select a stage. You will progress through 10 main levels in Chapter 1, starting from the *Tomb of Lilith* and pushing all the way to the *Throne of Gehenna* to defeat the Saintess of Light.
2.  **Tactical Prep:** Before battle, you arrange your **Cohorts** (teams) using the vassals you've collected.
3.  **Battle:** Engage in the Tower Defense combat to defend your Throne from human invaders.
4.  **Strengthening:** Use resources gained from battles or **Mandates** (daily stages) to level up and Ascend your Vassals.
5.  **Manifestation:** Spend premium resources in the **Manifest Vassals** (Gacha) portal to summon new and powerful demonic allies.

---

## 2. The Battlefield & HUD
When you enter a battle, your primary goal is to protect the **Throne** (your base HP) from reaching zero.

### The Sovereignty Bar (Resources)
*   **Authority Seals (Gold):** Your primary tactical resource. You spend these to deploy your Vassals onto the grid. They passively generate over time.
*   **Throne Stability:** Your Castle's HP. If enemies slip past your defenses and enter the Throne, this goes down.
*   **Commander Points (CP):** A gauge that charges up to let you cast powerful active "Maou Skills" globally.

### Camera Controls
*   **Rotate:** Rotate the 3D grid in 45-degree increments using HUD arrows (or a two-finger swipe on mobile).
*   **Tilt:** You can switch between `ISOMETRIC` (45°), `CINEMATIC` (30°), and `TACTICAL` (90° Top-Down) views depending on your preference.
*   **Zoom:** Pinch to zoom in and out (0.5x to 1.5x scale).

## 3. Deployment & Combat Mechanics
To win, you must strategically place your demonic forces (Vassals) to intercept human invaders (Knights, Adventurers, Mercenaries).

### Drag-and-Drop Deployment
*   **Select & Drag:** Grab a Vassal card from your bottom deployment bar and drag it onto the grid.
*   **Placement Validation:** The tile will glow **Cyan (Valid)** or **Red (Invalid)**. When you release, the Vassal is deployed and Authority Seals are consumed.
*   **Range Preview:** While dragging (or when you tap a deployed unit), you will see a glowing circle showing their attack range.

### Unit Archetypes & Placement Rules
1.  **The Frontline (Blockers):** *Classes: Bastion, Vanguard, Executioner.*
    *   **Placement:** Placed directly on **Path Tiles**.
    *   **Mechanic:** They physically "Block" enemies. When a human walks into their tile, the human's speed drops to 0 while they fight. Each unit has a specific `blockCount`. If more enemies arrive than they can block, the extras will walk right past!
2.  **The Overwatch (Ranged Support):** *Classes: Ranger, Warlock, Sage.*
    *   **Placement:** Must be placed on **High Ground Tiles**.
    *   **Mechanic:** They shoot down at the paths. Standard ground enemies cannot target or attack them. 
3.  **The Architects (Traps & Towers):**
    *   **Towers:** Placed on High Ground. Stationary and non-blocking.
    *   **Traps:** Placed on Path Tiles. They are untargetable and don't block enemies, but trigger devastating effects (Damage, Slows, Stuns) when walked over.

### Enemy Behaviors to Watch Out For
*   **Standard:** Walks the shortest path to the Throne, fighting blockers normally.
*   **Sappers:** These enemies prioritize destroying your Traps and Towers before moving on!
*   **Assassins:** They ignore all path costs and will slip right past your frontline blockers unless you use a unit that specifically "Taunts."
*   **Flying:** They fly in a straight line over the map to your Throne. Only your High Ground Rangers and Warlocks can shoot them down!

## 4. Maou Skills (Active Abilities)
As the Demon Lord, you aren't just watching. When your **Commander Points (CP)** gauge is full, you can unleash Sovereign Authority skills:
1.  **Select:** Tap the skill icon.
2.  **Aim:** 
    *   *Thunder:* Shows a 1.5-tile radius warning indicator.
    *   *Rally/Despair:* Show a global screen-border glow indicating a map-wide effect.
3.  **Execute:** Tap the target location on the field to unleash the skill and turn the tide of battle.

---

## 5. Damage & Elements
*   **Physical Damage:** Good against enemies with low armor. It mathematically subtracts the enemy's DEF from your ATK. 
*   **Magical / Dark Damage:** Good against heavily armored units like Heavy Vanguards. It ignores flat DEF and is only reduced by the enemy's magical Resistance percentage.
*   **Status Effects:** Utilize DoTs (Damage over Time) like Burn and Bleed to melt high HP enemies, or use Slow and Stun to keep enemies trapped inside your kill-zones.

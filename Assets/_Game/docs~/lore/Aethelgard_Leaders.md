# Leaders of Aethelgard

Aside from the Maou (who rules the **Kingdom of Drakmora**), the continent of Aethelgard is divided among three other major superpowers. These leaders serve as the primary antagonists, neutral parties, or potential reluctant allies throughout the campaign.

## 1. High Pontiff Valerius VI
- **Faction**: The Holy Empire of Sanctus
- **Territory**: Radiant lands of the South/East
- **Bio**: A fanatical, imposing figure who commands the world's largest religious military. Under his rule, the Empire captures and engineers "Hero Clones" to fight back against the demons. He wields blinding, oppressive holy magic and genuinely believes he speaks for absolute justice. 

## 2. Arch-Merchant Sterling
- **Faction**: The Trade Republic
- **Territory**: Coastal Midlands and Archipelagos
- **Bio**: The wealthiest individual on the continent. The Trade Republic cares little for the holy crusades of Sanctus, preferring instead to profit off the endless war. Sterling controls the flow of mana-crystals, naval transport, and heavily armed mercenary companies. Cunning, lavish, and entirely transaction-driven.

## 3. Frost-King Kaelen
- **Faction**: The Frostbound Clans
- **Territory**: The Northern Tundras (Bordering Drakmora's Frostbound Pines)
- **Bio**: A hulking, battle-scarred barbarian whose clans survive in the harshest environments of Aethelgard. Kaelen respects only strength. His people frequently skirmish with Drakmora's northern borders but hold a mutual disdain for the "soft" southerners of the Holy Empire.

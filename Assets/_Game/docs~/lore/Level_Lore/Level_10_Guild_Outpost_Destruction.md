# Level 10: Guild Outpost Destruction

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Ironpeak Mountains Base [X: 80, Y: 75]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Humans (Guild Captain & Elite Guards)
*   **Environment:** A heavily fortified stone outpost situated at the base of the mountain, serving as the Guild's primary staging ground for the region.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Completely eradicate the main Guild Outpost.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Guild Captain, Lilith.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*Riding the momentum of their victory in the mountain pass, the Sovereign's army descends upon the main Adventurer's Guild outpost. The Guild Captain desperately tries to hold the gates against the demonic onslaught, but wood and stone are no match for the Master of Gehenna. The Sovereign completely eradicates the outpost, removing the Guild's presence from the region and securing their hoarded magical artifacts.*

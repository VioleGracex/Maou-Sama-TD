# Level 11: The Weeping Swamps

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Sunken Valley [X: 110, Y: 40]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Shadow-Weavers (Forgotten Magic Race), Holy Crusaders (Human)
*   **Environment:** A dark, cursed swamp where the air is thick with ancient magic and hanging moss obscures the sky. The water glows faintly with bioluminescence.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Ambush the Crusaders to save the Shadow-Weaver race.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Lilith, Crusader Captain.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*Continuing east to expand their forces, the Sovereign enters the Weeping Swamps, home to the Shadow-Weavers—a forgotten race of powerful native mages. Upon arrival, they find the swamp ablaze with holy fire. The Church's Holy Crusaders have launched a crusade to "purify" the cursed land by slaughtering its inhabitants. Annoyed by the Church's self-righteous intrusion into Gehenna's territory, the Sovereign orders a brutal ambush against the Crusaders, saving the desperate Shadow-Weavers.*

## 🎭 Dialogue Sequence
### Intro
**Tina**: We have entered the Weeping Swamps [X: 110, Y: 40]. The air is thick with dark magic.
**Shadow-Weaver Elder**: Please... help us! The holy crusaders are burning our swamp! They call us heretics!
**Crusader Captain**: By the light of the Goddess, purge this tainted land! Burn the heretics to ash!
**Lilith**: Ah, the self-righteous Church. They intrude on our territory. Sovereign, let us teach them true fear.

### Outro
**Crusader Captain**: May the Goddess... forgive... my failure...
**Shadow-Weaver Elder**: You saved us from the cleansing fire. The legends were true... the Sovereign has returned to protect the forgotten races!
**Lilith**: Indeed. And in return, your magic belongs to us now.
**Shadow-Weaver Elder**: We gladly pledge our lives. Our Swamp Mages will fight by your side.

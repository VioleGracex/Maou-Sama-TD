# Level 12: The Sacred Tree

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** Heart of the Sunken Valley [X: 115, Y: 45]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Shadow-Weavers, Holy Crusaders (Siege Division)
*   **Environment:** A massive clearing in the swamp dominated by an ancient, glowing Sacred Tree, whose roots span the entire valley.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Defend the Sacred Tree against Crusader siege weapons.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Shadow-Weaver Elder, Drakmora Commander.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*Though the vanguard was defeated, the main Crusader force presses deeper into the swamp, bringing heavy siege weapons to bear against the Shadow-Weavers' Sacred Tree. If the tree falls, the valley's magic will die with it. The Sovereign, now commanding the Swamp Mages alongside their Mercenaries and Beastmen, establishes a defensive perimeter around the tree. They hold the line against wave after wave of holy fire, eventually breaking the Crusader siege.*

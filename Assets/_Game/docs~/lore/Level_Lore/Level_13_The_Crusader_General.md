# Level 13: The Crusader General

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** Eastern Swamp Borders [X: 120, Y: 45]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Humans (High Paladin & Royal Guard)
*   **Environment:** The charred, burning edges of the swamp where the Church's holy magic clashes violently with the native dark energy.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Defeat the Crusader General and uncover the truth of the 10 New Heroes.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Crusader General, Lilith.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*Enraged by the failure of his purification campaign, the high-ranking Crusader General takes the field personally. Radiating immense holy power, the General intends to smite the Sovereign. During the clash, the General boasts that he is merely a servant to one of the 10 New Heroes blessed by the Goddess. The Sovereign shatters the General's divine magic, completely breaking the Church's grip on the region and opening the final path to the Capital where the real war awaits.*

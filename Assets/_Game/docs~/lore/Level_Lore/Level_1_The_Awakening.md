# Level 1: The Awakening

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Ashen Wastes [X: 0, Y: 0]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Formless Shadows, Lesser Demons (Loyalists)
*   **Environment:** A ruined, subterranean cavern system devoid of natural light. The air is stagnant, smelling of ancient dust and dark magic.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Purge the infestation from the awakening chamber and begin restoring Gehenna's power.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Tina.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*After fifty years of slumber, the 13th Sovereign of Gehenna awakens in the deepest chamber of the Ashen Wastes. The realm has fallen into disrepair, and parasitic creatures known as Formless Shadows have infested the crypts, feeding on the lingering demonic aura. Guided by their loyal assistant, Tina, the Sovereign must reassert dominance over the awakening chamber and purge the vermin before setting out to reclaim their shattered empire.*

## 🎭 Dialogue Sequence
### Intro
**Tina**: Finally... your eternal slumber has concluded, Maou-Sama. The dungeon has missed its true master.

### Outro

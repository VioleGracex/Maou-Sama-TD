# Level 2: Tomb of Lilith

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Ashen Wastes [X: 5, Y: 15]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** High Demons (Sealed), Void Entities
*   **Environment:** An ornate, gothic mausoleum built from black marble, covered in glowing purple runes that are slowly fading.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Unseal the Void Matriarch to begin restoring Gehenna's power.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Tina, Lilith.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*To fully restore their fragmented authority, the Sovereign must awaken their most powerful general: Lilith, the Void Matriarch. Traveling deeper into the Wastes, they arrive at her grand tomb. Driven by a desire to reclaim their empire, the Sovereign breaks the final seals, awakening the Matriarch, and together they crush the fleeing void entities.*

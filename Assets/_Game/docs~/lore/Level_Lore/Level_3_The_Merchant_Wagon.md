# Level 3: The Merchant Wagon

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Whispering Woods [X: 20, Y: 35]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Humans (Merchants, Bandits, Mercenaries)
*   **Environment:** A dense, ancient forest on the borders of the Drakmora Kingdom. The trees are unnaturally tall, blocking out most of the sunlight.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Intervene in a bandit raid to secure early funds and recruit human allies.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Lilith, The Mercenary.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*Beginning their march toward the Drakmora Kingdom, the Sovereign and Lilith travel through the Whispering Woods. They stumble upon a heavily loaded human merchant wagon under siege by local bandits. While Lilith suggests ignoring the insignificant mortals, the Sovereign sees an opportunity to secure loot and indebted servants. They intervene, saving the merchants and catching the eye of a seasoned human Mercenary who pledges his shield to the Sovereign's cause.*

## 🎭 Dialogue Sequence
### Intro
**Tina**: Maou-Sama, we are nearing the borders of Drakmora. But look ahead! A merchant wagon is under heavy attack by bandits.
**Lilith**: Insignificant mortals squabbling. We should ignore them and press on. Or... perhaps dead bandits leave good loot? Let us intervene.

### Outro
**Bandit**: Run! They're monsters! Argh!
**Mercenary**: I don't know who you are, but you fight with terrifying precision. I am a mercenary sworn to protect these merchants, but...
**Mercenary**: Seeing your overwhelming strength... I pledge my shield to you, Sovereign. My blade is yours to command.

# Level 4: The Bandit Camp

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Whispering Woods [X: 25, Y: 40]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Humans (Bandits)
*   **Environment:** A fortified encampment hidden deep within a forest ravine, surrounded by crude wooden palisades and spiked traps.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Eradicate the bandit stronghold to secure massive war funds.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Lilith, The Mercenary.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*With the Mercenary now in their ranks, the Sovereign decides to eradicate the bandit threat entirely. The Mercenary leads the demonic forces to the bandit stronghold hidden deep in the forest. The Sovereign makes a brutal example of the thieves, slaughtering them to the last man. They claim the bandits' massive hoard of stolen wealth, securing the funds needed to finance their impending war against the Adventurer's Guild.*

## 🎭 Dialogue Sequence
### Intro
**Mercenary**: My Lord, the bandits who attacked us operate from a stronghold just ahead in the forest.
**Lilith**: A den of thieves? How quaint. We shall make an example of them. Leave none standing, and claim everything they have hoarded.

### Outro
**Mercenary**: The camp is decimated. We've secured a massive haul of their stolen wealth.
**Lilith**: Excellent. This loot will fund our campaign towards Drakmora. Let the remnants of this camp serve as a warning to those who oppose our march.

## 🎁 Spoils of War
*   **Rewards:** +1000 Gold (War Funds)
*   **Materials:** Timber, Stolen Weapons, Bandit Insignias

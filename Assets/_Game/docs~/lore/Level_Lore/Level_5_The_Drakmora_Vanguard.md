# Level 5: The Drakmora Vanguard

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Drakmora Borderlands [X: 35, Y: 45]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Humans (Drakmora Loyalists)
*   **Environment:** War-torn plains marking the edge of the sovereign's old domain. The ground is scarred by recent magical artillery and skirmishes.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Save the loyalist Vanguard and recruit them into your army.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Lilith, Drakmora Commander.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*Reaching the official borders of the Drakmora Kingdom, the Sovereign discovers a squad of loyalist Drakmora Infantry fighting a desperate battle. The Sovereign intervenes, unleashing their dark aura. Recognizing the terrifying, nostalgic power of their true master, the Drakmora Commander and his squad swear fealty on the spot.*

## 🎭 Dialogue Sequence
### Intro
**Tina**: We've reached the Drakmora border! But... the Drakmora Vanguard is already engaged in combat. They look battered.

**Drakmora Commander**: The Sovereign! The true master of Gehenna has returned! My squad and I swear fealty to you. We are ready to march under your banner once more.

# Level 6: Fort Vanguard (Mock Battle)

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Drakmora Borderlands [X: 45, Y: 50]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Humans (Loyalist Generals & High Guard)
*   **Environment:** A heavily fortified military encampment built from stone and ironwood, serving as the last bastion of true Drakmora loyalists.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Defeat the Loyalist Generals in a mock battle to prove your identity.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Lilith, Loyalist General.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*The Sovereign arrives at Fort Vanguard, the primary stronghold of the loyalist Generals who protected Drakmora during the 50-year slumber. However, the Generals are highly suspicious, believing the Sovereign to be an imposter or a trick by the Adventurer's Guild. To prove their identity and secure the Generals' trust, the Sovereign must participate in a tactical mock battle, displaying the undeniable strategic genius of the true Maou.*

## 🎭 Dialogue Sequence
### Intro
**Drakmora Commander**: Sovereign, we have arrived at Fort Vanguard [X: 45, Y: 50]. The remaining Loyalist Generals have fortified this position since your slumber.
**Loyalist General**: Halt! Who dares approach the Vanguard? The Drakmora Commander? And... no. It cannot be.
**Lilith**: How quickly mortals forget the aura of their true master. Kneel before the Sovereign.
**Loyalist General**: It's a trick! The Sovereign has been asleep for fifty years! You must prove your identity in a tactical mock battle. Show us the strategic genius of the true Maou!

### Outro
**Loyalist General**: Such overwhelming tactical prowess... the sheer presence... Forgive my insolence! The Sovereign has truly returned!
**Mercenary**: That was a brilliant display of command. We've secured their trust.
**Lilith**: Excellent. With the Generals on our side, our army grows. But we need better arms before we march on the capital. To the Ironpeak Mountains!

# Level 7: Khazad-Grom Infestation

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Ironpeak Mountains [X: 60, Y: 75]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Dwarves (Smiths & Miners)
*   **Environment:** Deep, echoing subterranean caverns illuminated by the orange glow of massive magma forges.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Clear the spider and golem infestation to secure dwarven weapons.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Lilith, Dwarf Forge-Master.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*Realizing their army lacks the heavy weaponry needed for a full-scale war, the Sovereign travels north to the legendary dwarven city of Khazad-Grom. The Dwarven Forge-Master is terrified by the Demon Lord's arrival but admits they cannot forge weapons anyway; the deep mines have been overrun by Rock Golems and Giant Spiders. Seeing a chance to indebt the dwarves, the Sovereign's forces descend into the mines to eradicate the infestation.*

### Intro
**Tina**: We've reached the dwarven caves of Khazad-Grom [X: 60, Y: 75], high in the Ironpeak Mountains!

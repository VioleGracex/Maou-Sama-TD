# Level 8: Highwind Peak Rescue

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Ironpeak Mountains [X: 70, Y: 85]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** The Highwind Beastmen (Avian & Feline Hybrids)
*   **Environment:** Treacherous, snow-capped cliffs and narrow passes where the howling winds make it impossible for normal humans to survive.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Rescue the Beastman Tribe from the Adventurer's Guild.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Lilith, Beastman Chieftain.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*The Sovereign and their vassals ascend into the freezing altitudes of the Ironpeak Mountains seeking the legendary Highwind Tribe. The goal is to recruit their unparalleled scouts and archers into the Sovereign's growing army. However, upon arriving, they find the Adventurer's Guild mercilessly slaughtering the Beastmen to claim the mountain's resources. Seeing an opportunity to forge a blood debt, the Sovereign commands their forces to eradicate the human invaders and save the tribe.*

# Level 9: Mountain Counter-Attack

## 🌍 Region & Inhabitants
*   **Region:** The Ironpeak Mountains [X: 75, Y: 80]
*   **Inhabitants (Race):** Humans (Adventurer's Guild Reinforcements)
*   **Environment:** A narrow, treacherous mountain pass known as the "Slaughter Chute," flanked by steep rocky walls.

## 🎯 Primary Objective & Characters
*   **Objective:** Ambush the Guild reinforcements ascending the mountain pass.
*   **Key Characters:** The Sovereign, Beastman Chieftain, Drakmora Commander.

## 📜 Chapter Narrative
*Enraged by their initial defeat at the village, the Adventurer's Guild dispatches a massive wave of heavily armed reinforcements up the mountain pass to wipe out the Beastmen entirely. Recognizing a fatal tactical error in the Guild's formation—funneling their entire force into a narrow choke point—the Sovereign and the newly recruited Beastman Archers set a devastating ambush, raining destruction down upon the humans.*

## 🎭 Dialogue Sequence
### Intro
**Beastman Chieftain**: Sovereign, the Guild is sending reinforcements up the mountain path! They intend to wipe us out.
**Drakmora Commander**: A foolish tactical blunder. They are funneling into a narrow pass.
**Lilith**: Let us rain destruction down upon them. Show them the fury of your new army, Sovereign.

### Outro
**Beastman Chieftain**: The reinforcements are crushed! Their bodies litter the pass.
**Mercenary**: They won't recover from a blow like that. Their main outpost is completely exposed now.
**Lilith**: Then let us descend and finish what they started. Leave no stone standing.

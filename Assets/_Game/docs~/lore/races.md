# The Races of Drakmora

In the kingdom of Drakmora, the Maou's forces constitute a unified but diverse array of deadly and elegant beings. The overarching design mandate emphasizes **chibi / anime stylings** with distinct armor, clean aesthetic contours, and a unified sense of purpose. Fodder units that do not fit the established aesthetics (such as goblins, slimes, or constructs) have been purged from the ranks.

---

## 1. Trueborn Demons
The ruling elite of the Abyss. They embody authoritative power and dark beauty.
- **Role**: Commanders, Lords/Ladies, elite casters and vanguards.
- **Aesthetic**: Elegant, noble, wearing finely crafted armor with obsidian and crimson accents, often featuring prominent, dark horns and majestic wings.

## 2. Demon Apostles
Fully demonized humans who have completely severed their ties to the light, reaching the absolute zenith of their dark potential.
- **Role**: Powerful defectors, generals, and specialized elites.
- **Aesthetic**: Tattered holy vestments mixed with dark spikes, floating sigils, and an aura of corrupted sanctity.

## 3. Demonized Humans
Lower-tier humans who defected and joined the demons, but failed to reach their full potential. They make up significant portions of the standing army.
- **Role**: Foot soldiers, frontline combatants, and standard militia.
- **Aesthetic**: Ragged human infantry gear interwoven with minor demonic traits, such as pale skin or blackened veins.

## 4. Vampires
Aristocratic stalkers of the night who strike from the shadows and control blood magic.
- **Role**: Assassins, Warlocks, and Blood Sages.
- **Aesthetic**: High collared cloaks, bat-like subtle motifs, dual-wielded rapiers or blood orbs, pale and elegant.

## 5. Succubus
A specialized offshoot of trueborn demons dedicated to temptation, subterfuge, and magical supremacy.
- **Role**: Seductresses, Support class, and mind-controllers.
- **Aesthetic**: Bat wings, spade-tipped tails, utilizing heart motifs and intricate magical staffs or whips.

## 6. Harpies
The masters of the sky, serving as swift aerial combatants and snipers.
- **Role**: Rangers, aerial bombardment, and scouts.
- **Aesthetic**: Feathery wings in place of arms or emerging from their backs, talon feet, utilizing elegant bows or wind blades.

## 7. Werewolves
The ferocious, primal wolf-kin whose strength in close-quarters combat is unmatched.
- **Role**: Bastions, durable Vanguards, and fierce trackers.
- **Aesthetic**: Wolf ears, thick tails, savage clawed gauntlets, and heavy, fur-lined armor.

## 8. Beastkin (Cat People)
Agile and cunning feline iterations of the shifter bloodline, renowned for their unmatched speed and trickery.
- **Role**: Assassins, Rangers, and swift attackers.
- **Aesthetic**: Cat ears, twin tails (Nekomata style), utilizing twin daggers, claws, or light crossbows.

## 9. The Undead
The reanimated corpses of the fallen, bound entirely to the will of necromantic masters.
- **Role**: Expendable vanguard, Bastions.
- **Aesthetic**: Shambling but well-equipped in rusted Drakmoran armor. Resilient and unyielding.

## 10. Liches
Ancient masters of death magic, preserving their intellect despite their skeletal or decaying forms.
- **Role**: Warlocks, summoners, and Tacticians.
- **Aesthetic**: Elaborate, tattered robes, floating grimoires, and glowing magical eyes.

## 11. Skeletons
The literal skeletal remains raised to form the bulk of the unthinking armies.
- **Role**: Common militia, archers.
- **Aesthetic**: Very clean, "cute" chibi skulls clad in oversized helmets or carrying tower shields.
